About Me

The Person Behind
The Data

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at Bennett University, currently working as a Data Analyst Intern at Lysandra Group of Companies in Gurgaon — where I help teams make smarter decisions by surfacing the stories hidden inside their data.

My toolkit spans Python, SQL, Excel, and YOLOv6. I've built a live intruder-detection system using computer vision and shipped a full campus marketplace called UniCore. I believe data without context is just noise — my job is to find the signal.

Outside of code, I'm a national-level competitive swimmer who represented Delhi at the National Games. That chapter taught me that grit, consistency, and a refusal to settle are skills you carry into every discipline.
